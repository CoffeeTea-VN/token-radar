How the price is made

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity. The peer multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in the same category whose token already trades in this snapshot — the market's own price for a dollar of TVL there. Growth rescales by the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% at a weight of 0.45. Maturity rescales by how long the protocol has run with no token recorded, on a square-root curve over four years: a launch priced after three proven years is not the same claim as one priced after three weeks.

Protocols whose token already trades are never ranked. They stand on the Already trading tab at the market's own values, and they are the evidence the multiples come from. The board also applies the watchlist's own product rule at every size, not only above its floor: a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades — WBTC, Binance staked ETH, USDT0 and their kin — is not a token that does not exist yet, so it is never ranked as a launch candidate, whatever its TVL. A protocol the snapshot records no token for may still have one its record does not carry; the board says “records no token”, never “has none”.
